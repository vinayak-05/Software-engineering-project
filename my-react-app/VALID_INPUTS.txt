========================================
VALID CROPS AND LOCATIONS FOR PRICE PREDICTION
========================================

HOW TO USE:
1. Enter CROP name in the "Crop Name" field
2. Enter LOCATION (district) in the "Location" field
3. Enter quantity in QUINTAL (1 quintal = 100 kg)

⚠️ IMPORTANT: Don't swap crop and location!
   ✅ Correct: crop=Tomato, location=Bangalore
   ❌ Wrong: crop=Bangalore, location=Tomato

⚠️ NOTE: Prices are predicted per QUINTAL, not per kg!
